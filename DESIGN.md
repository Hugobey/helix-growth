# Helix Growth Design System

Incremental identity, 2026-11-24. The cool white / Playfair system is retired. Do not import Fraunces, Outfit, chartreuse, or `#6366f1`.

`https://www.firstapp.fr/` is a pacing benchmark only. Do not import its orange, its type, or its French copy.

## Visual theme

A product-growth studio, scientific and technological, not a biotech company and not a SaaS dashboard. The helix is the recurring mark: two strands, a few nodes, one coral point. It stands for acquisition, activation, monetization, and retention as one system.

## Color

- Ground: `#F3EEE6`
- Surface: `#E7DFD2`
- Ink: `#1C1916`
- Mute: `#4F4942` (about 7.7:1 on ground)
- Accent: `#FF4D1C`
- Accent hover: `#F24618` (ink type on both stays above 4.5:1)
- Graphite panels: `#241F1B`
- Secondary on graphite: `#D9D0C3` (`foam`)
- Tide, used sparingly: `#3E6D88`

Accent is a fill, a node, or a large figure. Do not set small accent type on ground or on white. Inverse secondary text uses `foam` or `text-ground`, never the mute slate on graphite.

## Type

- Display: Instrument Serif, 400. Italic for one stressed phrase. Do not synthesize a bold.
- Body: Manrope, 400/500.
- Figures and labels: JetBrains Mono.

## Motion

One canvas helix, in the hero. It pauses off screen and draws a single frame when reduced motion is requested. Other helix marks are SVG. Hover lighting is a radial wash following the pointer, static when reduced motion is requested. Real client figures animate in place. They are never counted through false intermediate values.

## Proof that may be named

- Wizz: +$1M MRR. About $3M in October 2025 to about $4M in November 2025, after a monetization feature. The product had held near $3M. The feature was kept. $4M was the highest MRR. The chart plots only those two levels. Do not invent monthly points.
- Prose: +34% onboarding CVR versus the previous version. Relative, not 34 percentage points.
- Perifit: 2× paywall CVR, from more exposure and social proof on the existing value proposition.

Do not add metrics. Do not fake a Wizz interface. Official films: Prose `l-VOLNTUVTU`, Perifit `_OYmIexjX8U`, embedded from YouTube. Wizz has no embeddable official app demo.

## Offers

- App Growth Review, $350, one working day. Not an exhaustive audit. No implementation, no test execution, no ongoing support. Instrumentation is not included.
- Growth Sprint, $2,800, 7 days plus a call on day 30. Two a month. Deliverables stay the existing eight. The team ships.
- Monthly Growth Advisory, custom. No invented monthly price. Advisory only, not a build retainer.

Paid acquisition and building the product stay out of every engagement. Teaching counts on `/methodology` stay labeled illustrative.
